Questions

What teams ask before they move their support mail to Desk.

Email a question

Do customers see a new address?

No. Desk reads and sends from the address you already use, so replies look the same to them.

What if 2 people open one message?

The second person sees who is already replying, and their draft waits until the first reply is sent.

How many people can share one inbox?

Support teams of 2 to 20 people who share one inbox.

Is phone support part of Desk?

No. Desk handles email, chat and web forms. Phone calls stay in the tool you use now.

What is the price?

Ask for a demo and we will send the price for your team size with it.

Can we try Desk on our real inbox?

Yes. The demo uses your own email address, so you see your real messages in Desk.
